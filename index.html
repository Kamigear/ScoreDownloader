ScoreDownloader

Install the bookmarklet to download scores directly from MuseScore.

For MuseScore (Bookmarklet)

  1. Make sure your browser's Bookmarks Bar is visible (Cmd+Shift+B on Mac).
  2. Drag and drop the button below onto your Bookmarks Bar.
  3. Go to any score on musescore.com and click the bookmark to download!
MuseScore Downloader

For Songsterr (Browser Extension)

Because of strict browser security, Songsterr requires an extension to unlock.

  1. Download Tampermonkey from your browser's extension store (Chrome Web Store, Edge Add-ons, or Mac App Store).
  2. Make sure the Tampermonkey extension is enabled in your browser settings.
  3. Click here to install the Songsterr Script!
